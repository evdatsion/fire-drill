# Results

The bench applies the written rules from the skill references to 20 synthetic Drill Pack folders on the reference simulator (`sim/`, TypeScript, Node >= 20). It runs on a laptop, not a NAS; finalist day reports the measured score on a device. The simulator models the UGOS expert outputs: a fixture file's declared state determines its open-test verdict, its kind determines its tier, and hashes are computed over the synthetic content.

Score: **20/20** cases matched, **0** healthy folders flagged, two-drill sequence **matched**. Target: at least 18/20 with zero healthy folders flagged.
